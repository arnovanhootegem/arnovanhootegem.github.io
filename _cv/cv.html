Arno Van Hootegem

PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY

I am a sociologist working on health, social inequality, genetics and the welfare state. After my PhD at KU Leuven in Belgium, I held a postdoctoral fellowship at the University of Oslo, and I am currently a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Norwegian Institute of Public Health. I have published over 30 articles and chapters in esteemed sociological and social science journals and books, and I am an Associate Editor of Social Justice Research.
