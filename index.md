## About

I am a sociologist and postdoctoral researcher at the Centre for Fertility and Health at the Norwegian Institute of Public Health. My work sits at the intersection of genetics, health and social inequality: I study how genetic and social factors shape outcomes like mental health and labour-market careers, and how the public thinks about new reproductive and genetic technologies.

Before joining FHI, I did my PhD in sociology at the Centre for Sociological Research (CeSO), KU Leuven, where I studied public opinion on distributive justice, welfare attitudes and attitudes toward asylum policy across Europe.
