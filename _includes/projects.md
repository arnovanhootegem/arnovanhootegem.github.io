### YoungWork
Young adults' mental health and labour market exclusion: causes, consequences and trends. Using Norwegian register data, the project asks why mental health diagnoses among young adults are rising and how they affect education and work. Norwegian Institute of Public Health, 2024–2028. [Project page](https://www.fhi.no/en/nva-projects/ongoing/youngwork-young-adults-mental-health-and-labor-market-exclusion--causes-consequences-and-trends/)

### STRUGGLE
Gendered struggles and political outcomes, led by Staffan Kumlin at the University of Oslo and funded by the Research Council of Norway. My work in the project focuses on mental health. [Project page](https://www.sv.uio.no/isv/english/research/projects/struggle/index.html)
