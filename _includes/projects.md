### YoungWork
"Young adults' mental health and labour market exclusion: causes, consequences and trends", led by Bjørn-Atle Reme and Jonathan Wörn at the Norwegian Institute for Public Health. Using Norwegian register data, the project asks why mental health diagnoses among young adults are rising and how they affect education and work. I am Postdoctoral Fellow in this project. [Project page](https://www.fhi.no/en/nva-projects/ongoing/youngwork-young-adults-mental-health-and-labor-market-exclusion--causes-consequences-and-trends/)

### STRUGGLE
"Struggling Women and Men: Political-Democratic Gender Gaps in a High-Trust Welfare State", led by Staffan Kumlin at the University of Oslo and funded by the Research Council of Norway. My work in the project focuses on gender inequality in mental health in relation to political trust. [Project page](https://www.sv.uio.no/isv/english/research/projects/struggle/index.html)
